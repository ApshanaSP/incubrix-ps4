One post. Many small communities. Zero spam.

IncuBrix already publishes to the big platforms. This service takes a creator's post to the long tail: forums, communities, developer blogs, team chats, partner sites and video feeds. It adapts the post for each place, checks that place's rules first, and never posts twice.

Hard cases

The difficult situations from the brief. Each test creates its own throw-away place on a built-in test platform, causes the problem on purpose, and checks that the service does the right thing. Nothing real is touched. These are the same tests the batch runner uses.
