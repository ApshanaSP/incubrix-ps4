Long-tail distribution for creators

Post once. Reach the small communities that matter.

Creators already post to the big platforms. This service takes the same post to forums, communities, blogs, team chats and video feeds. For each place it adapts the post and checks that place's rules first, so nothing is posted where it isn't welcome.
